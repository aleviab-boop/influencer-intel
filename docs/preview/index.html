Influencer Intel

India-first influencer discovery, built AI-native. Type a campaign brief — or just an everyday plain-English prompt — and get ranked, real creators with live profile data, ready to reach out to.

Alevia Bandyopadhyay

1. Executive Summary

The one-line. A search engine for creators. You type in plain English — a full campaign brief (“beauty creators in Mumbai under 100k for a Durga Puja shoot”) or just a quick everyday prompt (“food vloggers in Kolkata”) — and the platform returns ranked, real influencers with live profile data, ready to reach out to. No filters to fight, no lists to buy.

Why it exists. Influencer marketing in India runs on gut feel, WhatsApp forwards and agency spreadsheets. Influencer Intel replaces that with an AI-native discovery engine tuned for India — regional languages, festival campaigns, and the long tail of creators that global tools simply don’t index.

Why it compounds. Every search is stored and re-ranked, so the underlying database grows on its own with each brief a user runs. The more it’s used, the more defensible it becomes.

2. Why It Matters

A booming market. Creator spend in India is scaling fast — but discovery is still manual, slow, and biased toward whoever an agency already knows.

Global tools miss India. They are built US-first. Vernacular creators, regional stars and festival-driven campaigns — exactly where Indian brand spend is going — fall through the cracks.

Brands overpay for stale data. Bought lists and spreadsheets still can’t answer the one question that matters: who actually fits this campaign, right now?

We answer in seconds. Live data, ranked for India, getting sharper every time someone searches.

Three discovery engines — an AI web-search pass, a live public-profile crawl, and the growing internal database — run in parallel on every brief, then results are merged, de-duplicated and ranked. The user just sees a clean, ranked list.

How the data is sourced

Free path first. Live profile data is pulled through the platform’s own crawler — a relay with a rotating pool of accounts and cookies. When it works, it works at zero marginal cost.

Apify as a pay-per-use fallback. When Instagram blocks a request (a 401, 403 or rate-limit), that specific profile automatically re-routes through Apify, a paid scraping service billed per profile — roughly a few cents each. No block means no bill, so cost scales with how often we’re blocked, not with how much we search.

Same answer either way. The engagement and ranking math is identical whichever path returns the data — the fallback is invisible to the user and only ever makes coverage more reliable.

7. About to Build: The Influencer Portal

The next build opens the other side of the market. Today the platform serves brands. Next, it opens to the creators themselves.

Creators log in — and see their future. An influencer connects their account and the platform predicts how their next reel or post will perform before they publish it — expected reach, likes and engagement, based on their own history and what’s working in their niche right now.

What a creator gets

FeatureWhat it does
Performance predictionForecast reach and engagement for a planned reel or post — so creators post what will land, not what they hope will.
Best-time & format guidanceWhen to post and which format (reel, carousel, static) is likely to perform for their specific audience.
Brand-fit signalsWhich brands and campaigns they’re a strong match for — feeding directly back into brand-side discovery.
Growth trackingA running view of what’s working, so creators see the effect of following the guidance.

Why this is the real unlock. The moment creators log in for predictions, they hand us first-party performance data no scraper can get. That sharpens the brand-side predictions, pulls both sides of the market onto one platform, and turns a discovery tool into a two-sided network — the flywheel that compounds.
